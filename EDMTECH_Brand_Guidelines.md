# EDMTECH Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

EDMTECH is a Portuguese technology and energy solutions company specializing in fullstack engineering development, integrating electronics and mechanical engineering. The brand positions itself as innovative, technologically advanced, and solution-oriented.

---

## 1. Logo

### Primary Logo

**Logo URL:** https://edmtech.pt/assets/imgs/logo_.png
**Dimensions:** 288 x 69 pixels

### Logo Composition

The EDMTECH logo is a **combination mark** consisting of two elements:

#### Icon (Logomark) - ⚠️ CORRECTED
- **Shape:** A complex **3D geometric crystal/gem shape** (NOT horizontal bars)
- **Appearance:** Faceted, three-dimensional hexagonal form with internal geometric patterns
- **Design:** Contains internal elements resembling a stylized "E" - appears as a crystalline structure
- **Symbolism:** Innovation, precision engineering, technological advancement, multifaceted solutions
- **Color:** **Solid RED (#E74F3D)** - NOT gradient

#### Wordmark (Logotype)
- **Text:** "EDMTECH" in custom letterforms (not a standard font)
- **Style:** Unique custom typography with distinctive character shapes
- **Color:** **White/Light Gray (#E5E7EB)** - NOT gradient

### ⚠️ Correction Note
Previous analysis incorrectly described the logo as three horizontal gradient bars. The actual logo features:
- Red 3D geometric icon (#E74F3D)
- White text on dark backgrounds (#E5E7EB)
- No gradients in the logo itself

### Logo Variations

| Variation | Background | Icon Color | Text Color | Usage |
|-----------|------------|------------|------------|-------|
| Primary | Dark/Black | Red (#E74F3D) | Light Gray (#E5E7EB) | Digital, headers |
| Reversed | White/Light | Red (#E74F3D) | Dark (#00263E or #000000) | Print, light backgrounds |
| Monochrome Dark | Any | Single dark color | Matching dark color | Single-color applications |
| Monochrome Light | Dark | White (#FFFFFF) | White (#FFFFFF) | Minimal applications |

### Logo Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the icon around all sides of the logo.

### Logo Don'ts

- Do not stretch or distort the logo
- Do not change the icon color (keep red #E74F3D)
- Do not change the text color (keep white/light gray)
- Do not rearrange elements
- Do not apply drop shadows or effects
- Do not use low-resolution versions

## 4. Visual Style

### Design Theme: Dark Mode Tech

The EDMTECH brand uses a **dark mode aesthetic** with high-contrast neon accents, conveying innovation, technology, and energy.

### Key Visual Elements

#### Backgrounds
- Deep black primary background (`#000000`)
- Subtle gradient glows (teal/cyan orbs) for ambient effect
- Glass-morphism effects on cards (subtle backdrop blur)
- Section backgrounds in darker navy (`#0B0E15`)

#### Cards & Containers
- Background: `#171717` or `#111111`
- Border-radius: 8-12px
- Border: None or subtle1px `#1F1F1F`
- Padding: 24-32px
- Hover: Subtle lift/shadow or border glow

#### Buttons

**Primary Button:**
- Background: Gradient (teal to blue)
- Text: White, Medium weight
- Border-radius: 6-8px
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Hover: Brightness increase or subtle glow

**Secondary/Outline Button:**
- Border: 2px solid primary teal
- Text: Teal or white
- Background: Transparent

#### Navigation Links
- No background
- Underline on hover (teal color)
- Smooth transition (0.2s ease)

### Decorative Elements

- Subtle gradient orbs/blurs in background
- Thin horizontal separator lines (1px, `#27272A`)
- Grid-based card layout
- Ambient gradient glows (teal/cyan orbs blurred)
- Subtle noise texture overlay (optional)

## 8. Accessibility Guidelines

### Color Contrast

- All text must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- White text on dark backgrounds: Minimum contrast 4.5:1
- Large text (24px+): Minimum contrast 3:1

### Focus States

- Visible focus indicators for all interactive elements
- Focus ring color: `#2195BF` (teal)
- Focus ring width: 2px

---

## 9. Dos and Don'ts

### Do

- Use the dark theme for digital applications
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Use gradient accents sparingly
- Keep typography clean and readable
- Use high contrast for important information
- Maintain consistent spacing (8px grid)

### Don't

- Use bright/neon colors as primary backgrounds
- Overcrowd layouts with too much content
- Use outdated or decorative fonts
- Apply heavy shadows or effects
- Mix too many accent colors
- Stretch or distort the logo
